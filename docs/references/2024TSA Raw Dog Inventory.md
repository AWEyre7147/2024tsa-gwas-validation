Analysis candidates receive private study IDs in the forms `GSP001` and `LR001`. Assignment is independent of outcome and does not preserve source-record order. The same dog retains its ID across both scenarios. Original identifiers, names, individual outcomes, and the ID crosswalk remain private. Pseudonymization does not make genotype data anonymous.
