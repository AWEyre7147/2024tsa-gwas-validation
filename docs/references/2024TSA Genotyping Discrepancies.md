# 2024TSA Genotyping Discrepancies

Public summary. Historical facility records raised sex and breed discrepancies, and the investigator recalls working with the facility on corrections. January 2024 is accepted as the primary resolved metadata under that assumption. Historical plate, sex, and ancestry flags are not cohort exclusions.

Exact metadata-to-genotype links are required. This acceptance does not independently verify every sample identity. Later genotype QC and population-structure checks can identify broad inconsistencies but cannot exclude every within-breed sample swap.

Individual discrepancy tables and original notes are retained in a private external backup. Public notes and notebooks contain no original identifiers or linked individual outcomes.
